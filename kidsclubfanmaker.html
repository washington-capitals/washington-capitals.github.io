About the Program

Caps Kids Club is the official fan club for young Washington Capitals fans! Designed for kids up to age 12 and their families, the program brings fans closer to the team through exclusive merchandise, memorable experiences, special events, and engaging content created just for young Caps fans and their families.

Frequently Asked Questions

Caps Kids Club Membership is available to children 12 and under.

A parent or guardian can sign up a child by creating an accouint and completing the parental verification steps. To begin the sign up process, visit capskidsclub.com/register.

Your Caps Kids Club membership starts the day you create your account and register for a membership. Your benefits will be active until that season’s membership ends or if you delete your account. The current membership begins in September 2026 and ends in June 2027.

There are three membership levels – FREE, Standard, and VIP. Each comes with different benefits.

Points can be earned by completing designated activities. Once you complete the activity, points are either automatically added to your account or you will be given a redemption code to add to your account and collect your points. Activities include reading books, attending or watching Caps games, and more.

Only standard and VIP members are able to redeem points. Points can be redeemed in the online prize store once logged in. To redeem a prize, you will select it and complete the necessary shipping information. Those points will then be deducted from your child’s account.

Points do not roll over year to year and expire at the end of the Capitals season.

Kits are shipped unless purchasing on-site at designated Capitals events.

Kits will begin shipping in early-October. Standard shipping is 3-5 days after your kit has been ordered.

All sales are final once your order has been submitted.
